# Controlled AI transitions

First state what the transition does for the story. Prefer a clean edit when it already communicates the change. Keep real people, products, events and results grounded in supplied evidence. Generated illustration must not imply that an undocumented meeting, facility or achievement occurred.

## Establish anchors before a paid run

Read `continuity-and-tests.md`. Identify the exact start and end frame, camera direction, screen position, scale, light, eyeline and objects that must remain stable. Build a cheap still sequence or local animatic and show the proposed timing. Define the required action in observable terms. A near-miss gesture does not satisfy a specified action.

Lock spelling, logos and important text in post-production rather than relying on generated glyphs. Use real product capture when demonstrating real software. Add a gentle camera move to a genuinely static capture only when it helps the edit; do not change the live page layout merely to fake camera motion.

## Keep authorization and cost explicit

Before uploading private source frames or recognizable people, verify the specific authorization and destination. Before paid generation, confirm the exact inputs, provider, current model schema, output settings, estimate and approved budget. Use the provider's current schema and estimate for the real request; a catalog base price is not the job price.

Record job ID, request settings and charged amount. Do not resubmit a paid job because polling timed out. Check the existing job first. Stop after a failed or unapproved attempt; propose the next change and its cost. This skill itself grants no upload, spend or publication permission.

## Judge the result against the anchor

Watch the full generated segment and dense strips around joins. Check geometry, face and hand stability, object persistence, camera drift, eye direction, exposure, start/end correspondence and the required action. Separate technical pass, visual judgement and client approval.

If chaining shots, extract the exact previous final frame by frame index after probing frame count. Do not trust an approximate seek from the end as an exact anchor. Check whether the next segment repeats that frame and handle the duplicate deliberately. Measure caption and overlay timing from the generated result, since the model may shift events.

After repeated compositing repairs introduce new seams or damaged edges, reconsider the shot design. A closer cut, a simpler action or a real plate can be better than another patch. Keep any degradation or unresolved requirement explicit.

Check software and asset licences before client use. Do not bundle proprietary tools or a third-party skill just because it is installed locally. Review the licence of the exact installed version before commercial use. This package includes no third-party skill code.
